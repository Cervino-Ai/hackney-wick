Working out where to eat and drink before a West Ham game at the London Stadium mostly comes down to which station you're arriving at. Come in via Hackney Wick and you're spoilt for breweries, bars and proper restaurants a short walk from the turnstiles. Here's where to go, and who each place suits.

## Crate Brewery

If you're arriving via Hackney Wick, Crate is the obvious first stop, and for good reason. It pairs a strong selection of lager and ale with pizza that holds its own, so you can line your stomach and get the first round in without changing venue.

**Who it suits:** Groups who want beer and proper food in one place before kick-off.

**Website:** [cratebrewery.com](https://www.cratebrewery.com/)

**Verdict:** The default Hackney Wick pre-match stop, and it earns the title.

## Howling Hops

Prefer your beer closer to the source? Howling Hops is the move after Crate. It's got a real brewery and tank bar feel, the kind of place where the beer is the point and everything else is secondary.

**Who it suits:** Beer people who'd rather drink in a brewery than a bar, and anyone doing a two-stop warm-up from Crate.

**Website:** [howlinghops.co.uk](https://www.howlinghops.co.uk/)

**Verdict:** The second pint of a well-planned Hackney Wick crawl.

## Lanterna

Over on Fish Island, about five minutes from the stadium, Lanterna is the pick if you want a sit-down meal rather than a pint and a slice. It runs a full menu alongside a well-stocked bar, so the drinkers and the diners in your group can both get what they came for.

**Who it suits:** Anyone treating match day as a proper day out, and mixed groups where not everyone wants to stand at a bar.

**Website:** [lanternafishisland.com](https://lanternafishisland.com/)

**Verdict:** The best bet for a real meal five minutes from the ground.

## INIS

Also on Fish Island, INIS is small and Irish-owned. It's certainly at the higher end on price, but it's well worth considering if you want somewhere with a bit more character than a busy match-day bar.

**Who it suits:** Smaller groups or couples happy to spend a bit more for something more personal.

**Website:** [inisfishisland.com](https://www.inisfishisland.com/)

**Verdict:** The splash-out option, and a good one.

## Two More Years

Two More Years is a straightforward place to drink and socialise before the game, with no pretensions about being anything else. It's at 7 Roach Road.

**Website:** [twomoreyears.co.uk](https://twomoreyears.co.uk/)
